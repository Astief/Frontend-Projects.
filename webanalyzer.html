About WebInsight

WebInsight was built with a single mission: to make website analysis accessible, fast, and actionable for developers, marketers, and business owners alike.

In today's digital landscape, a slow or poorly optimized website means lost revenue and poor user experience. Our robust analytics engine dives deep into the architecture of any public domain, parsing metrics across Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO.

Our Vision

To empower creators to build a faster, more secure, and inclusive web for everyone.

Whether you are debugging a complex web application or just making sure your portfolio site is running smoothly, WebInsight provides the data you need to make informed optimizations.
